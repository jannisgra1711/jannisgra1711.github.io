Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ClipShop is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and is used by its owner only. It has no public users. This page holds both its Terms of Service and its Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service

By connecting a TikTok account to ClipShop, you agree to these terms.

What it does

ClipShop creates short video clips and uploads the ones you approve to the TikTok account you connect. Nothing is uploaded without your approval.

Your responsibilities

  • You only upload content you own or are authorised to publish.
  • You follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines for everything you post.

No warranty

ClipShop is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. Its operator is not liable for uploads that fail, are rejected by TikTok, or are removed by TikTok.

Ending use

You can stop at any time by disconnecting TikTok in ClipShop's settings or by removing its access in your TikTok app. The Privacy Policy below says what that deletes.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The date above shows when they last changed.

Privacy Policy

This section describes what ClipShop does with data when its owner connects a TikTok account.

What ClipShop receives from TikTok

  • Your TikTok account identifier (open ID), display name and profile picture, so the app can show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, so it can upload videos you have approved.

ClipShop does not read your messages, followers, likes, viewing history or any other content of your account.

How it is used

The data is used only to upload videos you have chosen, either as drafts in your TikTok inbox or as posts you have approved, and to show the connected account in the app.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. Tokens are encrypted before they are saved. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

Selecting "Disconnect" in ClipShop's settings revokes the access token with TikTok and deletes the stored connection. You can also remove ClipShop's access at any time in your TikTok app's settings, under "Security" and then "Manage app permissions".
